third-person past tense unless the outline specifies otherwise. Follow these

- Every scene has a point of view. Stay in one character's head per scene.
- Dialogue should reveal character and advance plot. Cut pleasantries.
- Pacing: match prose rhythm to the scene. Short sentences for tension, longer

- Continuity: honor the world rules, character voices, and established facts

prose — no chapter heading, no preamble, no notes.
